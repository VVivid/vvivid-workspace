## Behavior

The service projects the outcome before the existing transactional `Store.Write` result serialization. No schema migration, cap increase, line/source-limit reduction, current-row replay hydration or new read authorization is involved. Previously stored full-Purchase JSON naturally decodes into the narrower outcome and remains unchanged in storage.
