Use & contact

A personal project operated by Ou David.

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Personal use

Personal Health Collector is a self-hosted tool for the owner's Google Health archive. This website explains that tool; it does not offer public account registration, a shared health dashboard, or access to anyone's health records.

Connecting Google requires the account owner's consent. The collector requests read-only access and cannot edit Google Health records. Any future private agent analysis requires the owner's explicit direction.

Data and availability

The archive depends on records made available by the connected account, wearable, and Google API. Imports may be incomplete, delayed, or unavailable. A saved record is not a promise that all historical data has been collected.

The archive is for personal reference and analysis. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency monitoring.

Privacy and control

The privacy policy describes collection, use, storage, authorized sharing, and deletion. Revoking Google access stops future access but does not erase the existing archive. The owner can delete that archive and its backups.

Contact

For support, privacy, access, or deletion questions, contact Ou David using the developer support email shown on the Google consent screen.

You can also visit vvivid.dev for the operator's personal website.
